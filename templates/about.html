ABOUT PETROL BUDDY

Fueling the Future with Doorstep Delivery

Petrol Buddy is reimagining how fuel reaches consumers — safer, smarter, and more convenient than traditional stations.

🎯

Our Mission

Petrol Buddy was built with a single goal: eliminate the inconvenience of refueling. Whether you're stuck in traffic, working from home, or managing a fleet, our platform connects you with certified fuel delivery — on your schedule, at your location.

  • Reduce commute time spent at petrol stations
  • Bring transparent, real-time pricing to consumers
  • Create employment for delivery professionals
  • Digitize the fuel supply chain end-to-end
🛡️

Safety Standards

Safety is at the core of everything we do. Every delivery partner is trained and every vehicle is equipped with fire suppression systems and spill containment kits.

  • Fuel sourced from authorized oil depots only
  • DOT-compliant mini-tankers with real-time monitoring
  • Mandatory background checks for all delivery partners
  • Spill-proof nozzles and automatic shutoff valves
  • Full insurance coverage on every delivery

Our Three-Tier Ecosystem

Petrol Buddy operates through three distinct user roles, each with a dedicated dashboard and set of capabilities.

👤

Customer

  • Browse live fuel prices
  • Place orders with delivery address
  • Track order status in real time
  • View order history and invoices
  • Submit feedback and ratings
  • Manage profile and preferences
🛠️

Admin

  • Full dashboard with analytics
  • Manage orders, assign delivery partners
  • Set and update fuel prices
  • Track inventory and stock levels
  • Manage customers and partners
  • View reports and revenue data
🚚

Delivery Partner

  • View assigned delivery orders
  • Update delivery status in real time
  • Navigate to customer locations
  • Access delivery history and stats
  • Manage profile and availability
  • Track earnings and performance
🎓

Academic Project Context

Petrol Buddy is developed as an academic capstone project to demonstrate full-stack web development skills. It showcases role-based access control, database design, real-time order management, and responsive UI using industry-standard technologies.

Python Flask MySQL Jinja2 HTML / CSS / JS Role-Based Auth REST APIs
