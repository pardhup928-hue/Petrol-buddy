Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common queries about Petrol Buddy.

Is doorstep fuel delivery legal?

Yes. Doorstep fuel delivery is permitted under regulations in many states when operated by licensed agencies using compliant mini-tankers. Petrol Buddy adheres to all applicable safety and transport guidelines.

Is there a minimum or maximum order limit?

The minimum order is 5 litres to ensure delivery efficiency. Maximum order limits depend on your area and vehicle tank capacity — typically up to 100 litres per order for individual customers.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), credit/debit cards, net banking, and cash on delivery. All digital transactions are secured with 256-bit encryption.

How can I track my order?

Once your order is confirmed, you can track its status in real time from your customer dashboard. You'll receive updates at each stage — confirmed, dispatched, out for delivery, and delivered.
